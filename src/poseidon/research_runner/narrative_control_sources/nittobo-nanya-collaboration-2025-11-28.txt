           Notice Regarding Collaboration with Nan Ya Plastics Corporation


Nitto Boseki Co., Ltd. (“Nittobo”) hereby announce that, at the Board of Directors meeting
held today, we resolved to collaborate with Nan Ya Plastics Corporation (Head Office:
Taiwan; hereinafter "Nan Ya Plastics").


We have been focusing on expanding our production capacity to meet the robust demand for
special glass resulting from the recent rapid surge in demand related to Generative AI. To
swiftly expand the supply of special glass cloth, we have decided to enter into a collaboration
with Nan Ya Plastics.


The manufacturing process for glass cloth consists of the yarn manufacturing process, which
produces the raw material, and the cloth processing process (weaving process and treatment
process). Regarding Yarn Processing, we are steadily expanding our yarn production capacity
through the commissioning of new melting furnaces and the conversion of furnaces for
general-purpose glass, and we will continue these efforts. As for Cloth Processing, we have
already decided and announced in August of this year to increase production facilities at the
Fukushima Business Center, with the new addition mainly focusing on introducing equipment
for the treatment process.
In addition, we have now decided to outsource a part of the weaving process for our special
glass cloth to Nan Ya Plastics.


Regarding Nan Ya Plastics and the Synergies of the Collaboration, Nan Ya Plastics is a
member of the Formosa Plastic Group, one of Taiwan's leading corporations, possessing a
long history and a strong business foundation. They operate four businesses: Electronic
Materials, Plastic Products, Chemical Products, and Polyester. Their Electronic Materials
business, which manufactures glass cloth, has one of the largest production capacities in the
world.
By leveraging Nan Ya Plastics' extensive weaving manufacturing capabilities—with its long
history in glass cloth production and large-scale, sophisticated resources in Taiwan (one of
our key production hubs)—and by concentrating our own investments on strengthening the
capacity for yarn manufacturing and cloth treatment manufacturing (which significantly
influence glass cloth quality), we will swiftly expand our supply capacity for special glass. It is
expected that approximately 20% of the total glass cloth supplied by the Nittobo Group will
be woven by Nan Ya Plastics by 2027.


Regarding our Next-Generation Low-Dielectric Glass (NER), we have traditionally supplied
glass cloth to various CCL companies through integrated production from yarn to cloth. This
time, we plan to supply NER yarn directly to Nan Ya Plastics, focusing specifically on their
CCL business.


We remain committed to expanding our business by meeting the robust demand for special
glass and will work together as one to establish our position as the "Global Niche No. 1" in the
electronic materials field.
